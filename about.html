About Afrisoft

Built in Malawi.
Engineered for every frontier.

We are a multidisciplinary technology company turning ambitious African ideas into practical products for learning, live production and creative expression.

One company. A growing product family.

Kuphanda brings capable AI closer to every learner. LivCast puts multi-camera production within reach. Afrisoft is the team connecting those ideas to real people.

Our direction

Local understanding.
Global standards.

Afrisoft starts with the realities around us: connectivity can be limited, professional equipment can be expensive, and exceptional talent deserves tools that do not hold it back.

That perspective shapes products that work offline, turn everyday devices into production systems, and make complex technology feel useful from the first interaction.

Built from lived experienceWe begin close to the communities and problems our products serve.
Engineering with purposeEvery capability must become a clear, dependable benefit for the user.
Designed to travelOur roots are in Malawi; our standards and ambitions are worldwide.
